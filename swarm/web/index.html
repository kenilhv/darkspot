        What this is built on

        • Relay placement: WMN-PSO node-placement fitness (Sakamoto et al., CISIS 2016); PSO per Kennedy & Eberhart 1995, Shi & Eberhart 1998. Simulation-stage academic work — swarm-intelligence-inspired, not a proven self-organising field mesh.
        • Routing: AntHocNet (Ducatelle, Di Caro, Gambardella, IJCIA 2005), eqs 1–8 as published; AODV per RFC 3561. No swarm routing protocol has real disaster field testing.
        • Allocation: ST-SR-IA (Gerkey & Matarić, IJRR 2004); Hungarian (Kuhn 1955 / Munkres 1957); sequential single-item auction (Koenig et al., AAAI 2006; Dias et al., Proc. IEEE 2006).
        • Ferry: message-ferry concept (Zhao, Ammar & Zegura, MobiHoc 2004). Tour is a nearest-neighbour heuristic. Not an airspace-deconflicted plan.
